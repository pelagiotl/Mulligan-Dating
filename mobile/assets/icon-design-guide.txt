MULLIGAN APP ICON - DESIGN GUIDE
=================================

SIZE: 1024x1024 pixels
FORMAT: PNG (no transparency)

DESIGN OPTION 1: Simple Heart
- Background: #8B1538 (burgundy)
- White heart icon in center
- Clean, minimalist

DESIGN OPTION 2: Card/Heart Combo  
- White background
- Burgundy heart (#8B1538) with playing card suit element
- Represents "second chance" concept

DESIGN OPTION 3: Letter "M"
- Burgundy background (#8B1538)
- White "M" letterform styled as a heart shape
- Modern, brandable

RECOMMENDED:
Option 1 or 3 for simplicity and recognizability

TOOLS:
- Use Figma (free) - create 1024x1024 artboard
- Or Canva - use App Icon template
- Export as PNG, save to: mobile/assets/icon.png

ANDROID ADAPTIVE ICON (adaptive-icon.png)
-----------------------------------------
Android uses a 1024x1024 foreground image but only shows the inner ~66% as a "safe zone"
(circle). Content outside this zone can be CUT OFF on some launchers (e.g. heart clipped).

RULE: Keep the heart (or main graphic) inside a circle of diameter ~66% of the canvas,
      centered. So for 1024x1024: safe circle diameter ≈ 676px, radius ≈ 338px from center.
      Scale the heart/logo DOWN and center it so it fits inside that circle.

- In Figma: add a 676x676 circle guide centered on the 1024 artboard; keep all important
  graphics inside the circle. Export as mobile/assets/adaptive-icon.png (1024x1024).
- Or: design at 1024x1024 with the main graphic occupying only the center ~676px width/height.
- Background color is set in app.json (adaptiveIcon.backgroundColor: "#8B1538"); the PNG
  can have transparency where the background will show.

This prevents the heart from being cut off on Android devices.
